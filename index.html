About Cowboy Safari

Saddle up for a wild adventure! Lasso, ride & tame exotic animals in Cowboy Safari. Build your sky zoo & conquer floating lands in this thrilling endless runner!

What is Cowboy Safari? An Adventure on the Backs of Wild Beasts

Forget everything you know about ordinary running games. Cowboy Safari, developed by Azgames, is a unique blend of intense, endless running and strategic zoo management. You are not just a cowboy; you are a beast tamer, a zoo curator, and an explorer all in one. Your playground is a breathtaking world of floating 3D pixel-art islands, from the sprawling Savannah to the treacherous Mountains, each teeming with exotic animals waiting to be tamed.

How to Play Cowboy Safari: Mastering the Perfect Leap

The goal is simple: ride as far as you can, tame as many animals as possible, and grow your sky-high zoo. But mastering it requires skill and strategy.

Basic Controls: Keeping Your Balance on the Wild Side

Arrow Keys (← →): Steer your animal to avoid obstacles and line up your next jump.
Spacebar is Your Lasso: This is your most important tool.
- Press & Hold: Aim your lasso at the next animal target.
- Release: Leap from your current mount to the new one.
- Pro Tip: Every animal has a unique "temperament." Watch for subtle signs, such as a slight twitch or a hop. Jumping just before they buck you off is the key to a long run.

Building Your Sky Zoo: The Secret to a Steady Gold Income

Every animal you tame returns to your magnificent floating zoo, held aloft by hot air balloons. This is your passive income engine.

Mastering Upgrades: Smart Investments for Maximum Profit

Don't upgrade randomly! Each animal habitat can be upgraded 8 times. Follow this priority:

  • Upgrades 1-3: Increase riding time (stamina).
  • Upgrade 4 (TOP PRIORITY): Boosts passive income from that habitat by 50%. Get this for every animal first.
  • Upgrades 5-8: Unlock special abilities like crate multipliers and increase chances of finding rare "shiny" animals.

Conquering Missions & Bosses: Your Path to Riches

Missions are your primary source of gold. You can hold three at a time.

Regular & Boss Missions: How to "Farm Gold" Efficiently

Regular Missions: Rewards scale with difficulty (e.g., 50 Gold in Savannah, 1350 Gold in Mountains).
GOLDEN STRATEGY: Select and complete multiple missions in a SINGLE run. A perfectly planned ride can earn you over 4,000 Gold.
Boss Missions: Extreme challenges with tough terrain. Upgrade the required animal to Level 4 or 5 before attempting these for a legendary reward.

Animal Guide: Choosing Your Perfect Steed

Know your mount! Each animal behaves differently:

  • Buffalo: The reliable all-rounder, perfect for beginners.
  • Zebra: Incredibly fast but hard to control, for expert riders.
  • Ostrich: The ultimate crate-smasher. Target crates when riding it.
  • Elephant: A tank. Hard to buck you off but turns slowly.
  • Lion: Ferocious and fast, the choice for seasoned pros.

Pro Tips to Become a Cowboy Legend

  1. Learn Their Patterns: Each animal gives a unique warning before bucking.
  2. Stay Calm, Don't Panic-Jump: Patience is key. Wait until you are in range.
  3. Always Tame New Species: Every new animal is a long-term investment for your zoo's income.
  4. Smash Those Crates: Don't ignore wooden crates, especially with animals that have crate multipliers.
  5. Stack Your Missions: One run, multiple completions = maximum gold efficiency.

FAQs

Q1: How do I unlock more cowboy characters?
A: A variety of cowboys can be unlocked through in-game luck-based spins or by completing special events.

Q2: How can I get rare animals?
A: By upgrading an animal's habitat to Level 8, you double the chance of finding its rare "shiny" version. Persistence is key!

Q3: What is the toughest challenge in the game?
A: The community often cites controlling a raging Elephant or conquering Boss Missions on mountain terrain as the most formidable challenges.

Q4: Is the "Cash Cow" worth buying?
A: The "Cash Cow" doubles your zoo's passive income, speeding up progression. It's a good option if you want to support the developers, but you can achieve everything through patience and skill.
